# Science Go Brrr

A simple but powerful Factorio mod that reduces all research costs to the absolute minimum, for insane builds

## Features

🔬 **Minimal Research Cost** — Every technology requires only 1 research pack
🌐 **Universal Compatibility** — Works with any mod's science packs automatically

Supports **Space Age**, **Space Exploration**, **Krastorio 2**, and any other science pack mod.


## Compatibility

- Works with **Factorio 2.0+**
- Works with any combination of mods that add technologies
- No conflicts with other research modification mods
